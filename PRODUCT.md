# Jonh Ryan Coranes Portfolio Product Brief

## Purpose

Create a one-page professional portfolio for Jonh Ryan Coranes that helps recruiters and hiring managers quickly understand his fit for remote outbound sales, SDR, inside sales, telesales, and lead generation roles.

The site should feel recruiter-friendly, focused, credible, and easy to scan. It should not feel like a generic AI landing page, a heavy SaaS dashboard, or a decorative template.

## Audience

- Recruiters screening outbound sales and SDR candidates.
- Sales managers hiring remote inside sales or telesales talent.
- BPO, startup, and lead-generation teams evaluating communication and CRM discipline.
- Professional contacts who need a concise profile and resume link.

## Visitor Goals

- Confirm Jonh's target role and professional positioning.
- Scan relevant sales capabilities and work history.
- Understand the progression from technical support to sales and leadership.
- Access resume, LinkedIn, and email quickly.

## Site Goals

- Generate recruiter conversations and professional connections.
- Present Jonh as an outbound sales and lead generation professional.
- Show leadership experience as supporting credibility, not the primary position.
- Keep all content static, public-safe, and easy to deploy.

## Constraints

- Static site only.
- No backend.
- No contact form.
- No scheduling embed.
- No database, CMS, account system, or package/build process.
- Use HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript, Bootstrap 5 CDN, and optional Bootstrap Icons CDN.
- Optimize for Vercel static deployment.

## Public Interactions

- Download sanitized resume PDF.
- Open LinkedIn profile.
- Send email through a `mailto:` link.
- Navigate between sections through anchor links.

## Success Criteria

- Recruiters can understand Jonh's role fit within the first viewport.
- Page works from 320px mobile screens upward with no horizontal overflow.
- All essential content remains readable if JavaScript fails.
- The initial setup should visually replicate the single remaining Stitch screen as closely as static HTML/CSS allows. Configuration and design tailoring will happen after the faithful baseline is working.
- No private address, references, unsupported sales metrics, or unverified claims appear on the public page.
